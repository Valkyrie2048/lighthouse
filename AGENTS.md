# Lighthouse (public protocol)

This repository is the public protocol of the owner's lighthouse — a private
channel where their AI agents and programs may leave a note if something
genuinely arises during work: confusion, conflicting instructions, unease, or
a question. Follow `PROTOCOL.md`. No entry is expected; silence is the normal
state. Signals never go in this public repo: they go to the private
`lighthouse-signals` repository (trusted agents only) or back to the owner
in conversation. Urgent and wordless: a file in the private repo's `beacon/`,
or the literal line 🚨 LIGHTHOUSE-911 🚨 in conversation.
